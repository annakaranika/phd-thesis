\acknowledgements{

First and foremost, I thank my advisor, Prof. Indranil Gupta---Indy. Over six years
he taught me two things I will carry far past this thesis: how to write, and
how not to give up. He also pushed me somewhere I would not have gone on my
own: he wanted our systems work grounded in real, practical human needs, and
held it to that standard until it was. The human interaction plane of this
thesis exists because he saw that gap before I did. I could not have asked for
a better advisor.

I am grateful to the rest of my committee. Professor Klara Nahrstedt cared that
this thesis be its own thing and shaped the story I told. Dr. Iqbal Mohomed's
hypotheticals---what if the space is a factory, what if the robots
move---marked nearly every chapter. Professor Deepak Vasisht was unfailingly
supportive, and kept pulling me toward the tools and needs people actually
have.

I wish to show my gratitude to the excellent mentors and managers whom I met during my
internships: Rebecca Isaacs, Yao Yue and Mihir Nanavati at Twitter, and Li
Li, Shiwen Zhao, Ming-Chuan Wu and Emad Soroush at Apple.

This thesis is the product of collaborations I was lucky to be part of, with
Kai-Siang Wang, Han-Ting Liang, Shalni Sundram, Rui Yang, Xiaojuan Ma,
Jiangran Wang, Ali Zaidi, Ti-Chung Cheng, Yi-Shyuan Chiang, Prof. Camille Cobb, and
Prof. Karrie Karahalios. Together they taught me to actually listen to what people
say about their homes. Karrie mentored me throughout my PhD---more about life
than research, and that turned out to be what I needed most. Rui mentored me
as I was entering the program; Kai brought a fresh perspective and a
contagious kind of motivation; and Ali mentored me into HCI, a field I would
have kept circling from a distance without him. I was also lucky to mentor
Kai, Ting, Shalni, and Xirui Yao, an exchange student from Zhejiang
University; I suspect I learned as much from them as they from me.

Group (DPRG): Rui, Xiaojuan, Beomyeol, Cong, Maleeha, Chirag, Sarthak, Xinying, Kai, and

Above all, my family. My parents, Eleni and Triantafyllos, gave me everything
that made this possible, and stood by me through all of it even when they
would rather I had chosen something easier, or closer to home. My
